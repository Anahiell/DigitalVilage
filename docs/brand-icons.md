# Brand illustrations

18 vector silhouettes are bundled in public/poster-model.js from Simple Icons 16.0.0 (https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/16.0.0/icons), CC0: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/16.0.0/LICENSE.md. No browser requests to an external icon service are made. Brand trademarks remain with their owners; the game does not claim endorsement. Unsupported brand tiles retain readable names. The canvas uses the same paths as the asset picker and PNG export.

These are educational usage scenarios. In-game contracts and fees do not grant real-world trademark or advertising permission.
